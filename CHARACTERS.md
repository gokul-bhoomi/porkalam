# Starting characters (draft for the owner's sign-off, 2026-11-07)

These follow [GROUND-RULES.md](GROUND-RULES.md) and [CLASS-RULES.md](CLASS-RULES.md). Strength is shown as small, medium or large, and exact numbers get tuned in play. Both fighters have the same power bar size and refill speed. Both also have the two ground-rule traps (planted trap and water trap).

**Signature moves have a long cast (~4 s).** The fighter stands still and can't move or block while a dramatic build-up plays. Any hit breaks it, and the cost is still spent.

## Krishna (God)
- **Movement:** slow. Can blink a short distance for a small power cost.
- **Block: Govardhana.** Krishna lifts the hill in front of himself. The longer he holds block, the bigger the hill and the more damage it absorbs.

**Basic attacks**

| Move | Range | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Flute note | Mid | Small sound-wave projectile, small damage |
| Palm push | Close | Small damage, big knockback |

**Special moves**

| Move | Range | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sudarshana Chakra | Anywhere | Large | Homing disc, medium damage |
| Venu Ganam | Mid | Medium | 2 s cast, then the King is held still for 3 s |
| Signature: Vishwaroopam | Anywhere | Very large + health | Dramatic scene. Large damage across the whole arena |
| Ultimate: Brahmastra | Anywhere | All power. Requires health below 5% | ~5 s chant that a hit breaks. Both fighters reach 0 health, so it's a draw |

**Tuning (owner, 2026-11-07):** the flute note costs 3 power (was 2). Krishna was winning mostly by spamming notes, which beat the King's basic shot in every clash; the higher cost pushes him toward his specials.

Arena changes (Yamuna flood, Maya) and Call a King are left out of Krishna's first version. The God class still allows them for later.

## Raja Raja Chola (King, bow)
- **Movement:** fast.
- **Block: arrow wall.** Arrows stick into the ground in front of him. The longer he holds block, the more arrows and the more damage the wall absorbs.

**Basic attacks**

| Move | Range | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Shot | Mid/long | Tap for a quick arrow (small damage). Hold to charge (more damage the longer it's held) |
| Knife attack | Close | Small damage, knockback |

**Special moves**

| Move | Range | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Split shot | Mid | Small | 3 arrows fan out |
| Pinning arrow | Mid/long | Medium | Pins the opponent in place for ~1.5 s |
| Arrow rain | Mid | Medium | Arrows fall on an area |
| Signature: Tiger Arrow | Long | Very large + health | Dramatic scene. A tiger spirit rides the arrow. It pierces blocks and helpers for large damage |
| Meditation | n/a | Time | A single-use boon from a random god |

**Boons.** One random boon per level, each single-use. Praying to Krishna is left out for now.

| God | 1st (small) | 2nd (medium) | 3rd+ (big) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shiva | Third-eye spark (fire shot) | Damaru: stuns the opponent | Pashupatastra: huge damage from anywhere |
| Murugan | Vel throw | Peacock dash: a quick escape that can't be hit | Shakti Vel: pierces any block |
| Brahma | Kamandalu: restores 10% health | Lotus seat: next meditation is twice as fast | Brahmastra: both fighters reach 0 health, so it's a draw |
