# Starting characters (owner, 2026-11-08)

These follow [GROUND-RULES.md](GROUND-RULES.md) and [CLASS-RULES.md](CLASS-RULES.md). Strength is shown as small, medium or large, and exact numbers get tuned in play. Both fighters have the same power bar size and refill speed.

Both fighters are **original characters**. The game is inspired by Hindu mythology: it shows in their dress and the arena, and the real gods appear only as givers of boons. (They replaced Krishna and Raja Raja Chola on 2026-11-08.)

**Signature moves have a long cast (~4 s).** The fighter stands still and can't move or block. Any hit breaks it, and the cost is still spent. When the cast completes, a power-up plays in the arena itself: the camera closes in, the world around the sand island becomes the power, and the move goes off.

## Siddhan (Mage)
A young wandering mystic-sorcerer: saffron and indigo robes, rudraksha beads, a topknot, and a staff topped with a brass bell. His magic glows teal.
- **Movement:** slow. Can blink a short distance for a small power cost.
- **Block: Rising earth.** A mound of earth rises in front of him. The longer he holds block, the bigger it grows and the more damage it absorbs.

| Move | Range | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mantra bolt (basic) | Mid | Small (3 power) | A glowing teal orb, small damage |
| Palm push (basic) | Close | Small | Small damage, big knockback |
| Spinning chakra | Anywhere | Large | Homing disc, medium damage |
| Binding chant | Mid | Medium | 2 s cast, then the opponent is held still for 3 s |
| Blink | Anywhere | Small | Short teleport |
| Signature: Thousand-Arm Form | Anywhere | Very large + health | Power-up in the arena. Large damage across the whole arena |
| Meditation | n/a | Time | Fills devotion behind a barrier |

## Veeran (Warrior, bow)
A South Indian archer-warrior: bronze and crimson armour, a tiger-stripe sash, a cloth turban with a metal band, a longbow and a curved knife.
- **Movement:** fast.
- **Block: arrow wall.** Arrows stick into the ground in front of him. The longer he holds block, the more arrows and the more damage the wall absorbs.

| Move | Range | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shot (basic) | Mid/long | Small (3 power) | Tap for a quick arrow. Hold to charge (more damage the longer it's held). Arrows fly slow enough to dodge by moving (owner, 2026-11-08: arrow spam was too strong) |
| Knife attack (basic) | Close | Small | Small damage, knockback |
| Split shot | Mid | Small | 3 arrows fan out |
| Pinning arrow | Mid/long | Medium | Pins the opponent in place for ~1.5 s |
| Arrow rain | Mid | Medium | Arrows fall on an area |
| Signature: Gaja Arrow | Long | Very large + health | Power-up in the arena. A spectral war elephant rides the arrow. It pierces blocks and helpers for large damage |
| Meditation | n/a | Time | Fills devotion behind a barrier |

## Boons (either fighter, ground rule 11)
Spending devotion buys a boon from a random god: 3 segments = small, 6 = medium, 10 = divine weapon. Each boon is single-use.

| God | Small (3) | Medium (6) | Divine weapon (10) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shiva | Third-eye spark (fire shot) | Damaru: stuns the opponent | Pashupatastra: huge damage from anywhere |
| Murugan | Vel throw | Peacock dash: a quick escape that can't be hit | Shakti Vel: pierces any block |
| Brahma | Kamandalu: restores 10% health | Lotus seat: next meditation is twice as fast | Brahmastra: both fighters reach 0 health, so it's a draw |
| Krishna (joined 2026-11-08) | Flute charm: holds the opponent still for 1.5 s | Sudarshana Chakra: homing disc | Vishwaroopam: the cosmic form, large damage across the whole arena |

When a boon is invoked, the fight slows down for about 1.5 s, the god's light comes down on the caster and, for small and medium boons, the god appears as a vast vision over the arena, blessing. Boon attacks fly larger, with a glowing trail. Divine weapons go through a meditation barrier and play a power-up with the god's painting. Strong hits crack the sand, and power moves throw the river up round the island. Brahmastra is rare unless the caster's health is low (about 5% above 35% health, 20% at 15-35%, 50% at 15% or less).
