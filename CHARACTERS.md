# Starting characters (owner, 2026-11-08)

These follow [GROUND-RULES.md](GROUND-RULES.md) and [CLASS-RULES.md](CLASS-RULES.md). Strength is shown as small, medium or large, and exact numbers get tuned in play. Both fighters have the same power bar size and refill speed.

Both fighters are **original characters**. The game is inspired by Indian mythology: it shows in their dress and the arena, and the real gods appear only as givers of boons. (They replaced Krishna and Raja Raja Chola on 2026-11-08.)

**Signature moves have a 2 s cast** (was ~4 s; owner, 2026-11-09), and both signatures do the same damage (35%). The fighter stands still and can't move or block. Any hit breaks it, and the cost is still spent. When the cast completes, a power-up plays in the arena itself: the camera closes in, the world around the sand island becomes the power, and the move goes off.

## Siddhan (Mage)
A young wandering mystic-sorcerer: saffron and indigo robes, rudraksha beads, a topknot, and a staff topped with a brass bell. His magic glows teal.
- **Movement:** slow. Can blink a short distance for a small power cost.
- **Identity (owner, 2026-11-14):** control, and magic that finds you, but slow.
- **Block: Rising earth.** A mound of earth rises in front of him. The longer he holds block, the bigger it grows and the more damage it absorbs.

| Move | Range | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mantra bolt (basic) | Mid | Small (3 power) | A glowing teal orb. Tap for a quick bolt, hold to charge (more damage and a bigger orb, up to 11%). Weakens past 80, to half by 150 |
| Palm push (basic) | Close | Small | Small damage, big knockback |
| Spinning chakra | Anywhere | Medium (18 power; was 30, owner 2026-11-14) | Homing disc, medium damage: his answer to a fast opponent at range |
| Fly (replaced the binding chant, owner 2026-11-14) | Anywhere | Small to take off, then power every second | Press to rise (0.3 s), press again to land. Flies fast and still attacks. Melee and arrow rain miss him; shots still hit (ground rule 17) |
| Blink | Anywhere | Small | Short teleport |
| Signature: Thousand-Arm Form | Anywhere | Very large + health | Power-up in the arena. Large damage across the whole arena |
| Meditation | n/a | Time | Fills devotion behind a barrier |

## Veeran (Warrior, bow)
A South Indian archer-warrior: bronze and crimson armour, a tiger-stripe sash, a cloth turban with a metal band, a longbow and a curved knife.
- **Movement:** fast.
- **Identity (owner, 2026-11-14):** speed and aimed arrows, but the arrows can be dodged, walls stop them, and they weaken from far away.
- **Block: arrow wall.** Arrows stick into the ground in front of him. The longer he holds block, the more arrows and the more damage the wall absorbs.

| Move | Range | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shot (basic) | Mid/long | Small (3 power) | Tap for a quick arrow. Hold to charge (more damage the longer it's held). Arrows fly slow enough to dodge by moving (owner, 2026-11-08: arrow spam was too strong). Full damage up to 100, then weaker: half by 200 (owner, 2026-11-14) |
| Knife attack (basic) | Close | Small | Small damage, knockback |
| Jump (replaced split shot, owner 2026-11-14) | Anywhere | Small | A 0.5 s hop the way you hold. Shots, melee and arrow rain pass under him; homing moves find him after he lands (ground rule 17) |
| Pinning arrow | Mid/long | Medium | Pins the opponent in place for ~1.5 s |
| Arrow rain | Mid | Medium | Arrows fall on an area |
| Signature: Gaja Arrow | Long | Very large + health | Power-up in the arena. A spectral war elephant rides the arrow. It pierces blocks and helpers for large damage |
| Meditation | n/a | Time | Fills devotion behind a barrier |

## Boons (either fighter, ground rule 11)
Two specials spend devotion to ask a random god for a boon: 4 segments = small, 8 = divine weapon (owner, 2026-11-14: two levels, each worth the health lost sitting for it). The god reveals the boon and it's held (one at a time); two specials again use it, now or later. Each boon is single-use.

| God | Small (4) | Divine weapon (8) |
|---|---|---|
| Shiva | Shiva's Tandava: the island splits for 8 s, 10% damage; the opponent's half sinks into lava, 2% a second on the ground there until they jump or fly across (about 26% if they stay) | Pashupatastra: 45% damage from anywhere |
| Murugan | Vel throw: a spear, 16% damage | Shakti Vel: 40% damage, pierces any block |
| Brahma | Kamandalu: restores 18% health | Brahmastra: both fighters reach 0 health, so it's a draw |
| Krishna | (none) | Vishwaroopam: 40% damage across the whole arena |

Owner, 2026-11-14: the medium level was removed, along with Third-eye spark, Sudarshana Chakra (Siddhan already has a chakra) and Lotus seat. Small boons were raised to 16-18% so they're worth sitting for.

Owner, 2026-11-08: Flute charm and Peacock dash were removed. A boon's attack always finds the opponent: it homes in, works from any distance and passes walls; only another attack or boon meeting it can weaken or cancel it. When a god grants a boon, a card shows its name, what it does and the keys to use it.

When a boon is invoked, the fight slows down for about 1.5 s, the god's light comes down on the caster and, for small boons, the god appears as a vast vision over the arena, blessing. Boon attacks fly larger, with a glowing trail. The god rises in the sky behind the island, never over the sand, so attacks stay visible. Bells and a choir sound when a god answers; a divine weapon adds a conch. Damage raised by the owner on 2026-11-08: divine weapons 45 / 35 / 40, signatures 35. Where they land, the arena shatters. Divine weapons go through a meditation barrier and play a power-up with the god's painting. Strong hits crack the sand, and power moves throw the river up round the island. Brahmastra is rare unless the caster's health is low (about 5% above 35% health, 20% at 15-35%, 50% at 15% or less).
