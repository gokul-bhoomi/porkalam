# Class rules (agreed 2026-11-07, changed by the owner 2026-11-08)

These rules sit on top of [GROUND-RULES.md](GROUND-RULES.md) and never contradict it.

**Theme (owner, 2026-11-08):** the game is *inspired by Indian mythology*. The fighters are original characters: the mythology lives in the arena, costumes and ornament. The real gods (Shiva, Murugan, Brahma and Krishna) appear only as givers of boons.

There are **two classes, Mages and Warriors**. There are no Monsters (removed to keep the game simple).

## Shared by both classes
- **Power bar size and refill speed are the same for every class.**
- **Each character has their own special block.** The ground rules still apply: holding block longer makes it stronger.
- **Every character has the same number of basic moves: 2 basic attacks + 1 block.**
- **Class move: meditation**, for both classes (ground rule 11). The god who answers is random; nobody picks one.
- **Boons are strictly single-use**: each one is paid for with devotion.
- **Brahmastra** is a divine-weapon boon that only Brahma grants. Both fighters reach 0 health at once, which makes it a draw. It's rare (about 5% of divine weapons) unless the caster's health is low: about 20% at 35% health or less, about 50% at 15% or less. (Owner, 2026-11-08.) Its cast takes 1.5 s, only a little slower than the other divine weapons (owner, 2026-11-15). Once it goes off it hangs in the sky for 6.6 s, with both fighters held, before its sun lands. Whoever starts first has the advantage: if the other fighter's Brahmastra was already being cast when the first one finished casting, and theirs goes off before the sun lands, Shiva takes both back, nobody is hurt and the fight goes on. Starting one any later is too late (owner, 2026-11-15).

## Mage
- **Movement:** slow, can't run. A short blink costs power.
- **Basic moves:** mostly mid/long range. Weak up close.
- **Special moves:** may have *anywhere* range.
- **Arena changes:** may apply arena changes later, one at a time (not in this version).

## Warrior
- **Movement:** fast.
- **Weapon:** **one weapon per Warrior.** Basic and special moves come from it.
- **No arena changes.**
