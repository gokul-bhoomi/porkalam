Porkalam music sketches

Three directions for the fight music, about 25 seconds per loop. All three play the same tune so you can compare the sound. Everything is synthesized, with no audio files.

A · Thavil mass

146 BPM

Tamil film fight music: fast thavil and parai drums, a brass lead, and a drone underneath.

B · Arcade raga

152 BPM

A 90s arcade fighter sound: punchy octave synth bass, an FM brass lead, an arpeggio, and gated kick and snare.

C · Hybrid (A + B)

148 BPM

Arcade bass and backbeat for drive, thavil fills on top, and the brass lead.
